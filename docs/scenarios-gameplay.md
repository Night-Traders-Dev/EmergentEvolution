# Scenarios & Gameplay

12 guided scenarios with multi-step objectives across four categories. Each scenario features a
narrative storyline and 3&ndash;4 sequential tasks that guide you through progressively deeper
physics concepts.

## Multi-Task System

Each non-sandbox scenario has multiple sequential tasks. As you complete each task:

1. **Narrative text** fades in, providing story context for the next objective
2. The **Goal HUD** updates with the new task and progress counter (e.g., "Task 2/4")
3. **Progress dots** below the HUD show completed, current, and remaining tasks
4. **Hint text** appears below the objective to guide you

When all tasks are complete, the HUD shows **"SCENARIO COMPLETE!"** and the scenario is marked
as finished in the scenario menu.

## Scenario Details

### Nuclear: First Light &mdash; *The Dawn of Stars*

Observe hydrogen atoms interacting and producing the universe's first photons.

| Task | Objective | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Observe the primordial hydrogen | Watch the particles |
| 2 | Bring atoms close together | Protons capture electrons to form hydrogen atoms |
| 3 | Create a photon | Energetic collisions between atoms can emit photons |

### Nuclear: Hydrogen Factory &mdash; *Building the Periodic Table*

Build complete hydrogen atoms with bound electrons.

| Task | Objective | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build your first hydrogen atom | Spawn protons near electrons |
| 2 | Build 3 hydrogen atoms | Keep spawning protons and electrons nearby |
| 3 | Build 5 hydrogen atoms | Electron capture takes time at low temperature |

### Nuclear: Solar Core &mdash; *Heart of a Star*

Trigger nuclear fusion in the heart of a star.

| Task | Objective | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raise temperature above 1 million K | Use the thermostat coupling slider |
| 2 | Trigger your first fusion reaction | High temperature + dense protons = fusion |
| 3 | Create Helium through fusion | Fusion of protons produces helium nuclei |
| 4 | Trigger 10 fusion reactions | Dense, hot environments produce continuous fusion |

### Nuclear: Chain Reaction &mdash; *Critical Mass*

Cause a fission chain reaction from heavy nuclei.

| Task | Objective | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Observe a fission event | Heavy nuclei + fast neutrons = fission |
| 2 | Trigger a chain reaction (3+ in 1s) | Concentrate heavy nuclei together |
| 3 | Produce 20 fission events total | Each split releases more neutrons |

### Nuclear: Antimatter Lab &mdash; *Through the Looking Glass*

Create and observe particle-antiparticle annihilation.

| Task | Objective | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create a positron | High-energy collisions produce positron-electron pairs |
| 2 | Create an antiproton | Even higher energies are needed |
| 3 | Witness annihilation | Bring particles and antiparticles together |
| 4 | Trigger 10 annihilations | More matter-antimatter pairs = more events |

|---|---|---|

Discover the menagerie of fundamental particles.

| Task | Objective | Hint |
|---|---|---|

### Cosmology: Stellar Nucleosynthesis &mdash; *Forging the Elements*

Build elements up to Iron through fusion chains.

| Task | Objective | Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create Helium (Z=2) | Proton-proton fusion chain produces helium |

| 4 | Create Iron (Z=26) or heavier | Extreme temperature fuses lighter into heavier |

## Goal HUD

The scenario HUD appears at the top-center of the screen below the stats bar:

- **Active**: `Scenario Name | Task X/Y | Current objective`
- **Task complete**: Blue flash with "Task Complete!" text, auto-advances after 3 seconds
- **All complete**: Green border with "SCENARIO COMPLETE!" text

Narrative text from the storyline fades in and out over 5 seconds when each task begins.
A small **X** button on the right side of the HUD allows ending the scenario early.

## Scenario Cards

The scenario selection menu (accessible from the pause menu) shows:

- Category-colored border and badge
- Scenario name and storyline subtitle
- Description text
- Task count badge (e.g., "4 Tasks")
- Completion checkmark for previously completed scenarios
- **Start** / **Replay** button

| Chemist | Complete all Chemistry scenarios |
